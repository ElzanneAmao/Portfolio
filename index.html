Most of the marketing conversation about AI is about producing more, faster. Almost none of it is about what that's doing to the trust a business has spent years building with the people it serves.

Services

Work is moving faster since your team started using AI. But you don't have a clear picture of what the system as a whole is producing, what it can't produce, and where your team still needs to be the one making the call. My starting point is always diagnostic: I help you understand what's happening before I propose changes. The goal is a content operation where AI does what it's good at, your team does what it's good at, and the output sounds like your brand.

There are three ways an engagement usually starts

AI content operations audit

If you can tell something is off with your content since AI entered the workflow but you're not sure what, this is where to start. I produce a written read on what's working, what isn't, and where the highest-leverage changes are. The deliverable is yours to use whether or not you do more work with me.

Inquire →

AI-integrated content system design

If you know what needs to change and you need someone to design the system, this is the build engagement. I design or rebuild your content operation so your team knows what AI handles, what they handle, and how the pieces fit together. Usually follows an audit, but can stand alone.

Inquire →

AI advisory for SME leadership

If you don't need a project but you do need someone to think with, this is a retainer. Monthly check-ins, async availability between them, and a quarterly written read on how your AI integration is holding up against the business you're building. Three months minimum.

Inquire →

If you're not sure which of these fits, that's fine. Email me and describe what you're working on — I'd rather have a conversation than fit your situation into a pre-defined package.
