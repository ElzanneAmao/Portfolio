Most of the marketing conversation about AI is about producing more, faster. Almost none of it is about what that's doing to the trust a business has spent years building with the people it serves.

What I do

I work with small and mid-sized companies trying to figure out what AI is doing inside their content and communication function — and what it should be doing, given the work they're actually trying to get done. Most have stitched AI into parts of their content operation without a coherent picture of what the system as a whole is producing, what it can't produce, and where the humans still need to stay involved. That's the question I work on.

I'm a strategic communications generalist with sixteen years behind me in education marketing, technology, and communications, and a Master's in Strategic Communication from the University of Johannesburg. The way I work is diagnostic before it's anything else: I want to understand what's actually happening in your content function before I propose changes to it.

There are three ways an engagement usually starts

AI content operations audit

A diagnostic engagement that looks at your AI-integrated content function as it is now and produces a clear written read on what it does well, what it doesn't, where the humans need to stay in the loop, and where the highest-leverage changes are. The deliverable is a written audit you can use to make decisions — whether or not those decisions involve more work with me. The sensible starting point if you know something isn't working but aren't sure what.

Inquire →

AI-integrated content system design

A build engagement that designs or rebuilds your content operations system, including the human-in-the-loop architecture that determines what AI handles and where your team still needs to stay involved. This is the deeper engagement. It usually follows an audit, but it can stand alone if you already know what you want built.

Inquire →

AI advisory for SME leadership

A retainer-style engagement for leaders who need a thinking partner on AI decisions but don't need a full-time strategist. Monthly check-ins, async availability for the questions that come up between them, and a quarterly written read on how your AI integration is holding up against the business you're actually trying to build. Three months minimum.

Inquire →

If none of these is quite the fit but you think the work might be useful, the right move is to email me and describe what you're working on. I'd rather have a conversation than fit your situation into a pre-defined package.
