AI-integrated content system design

A build engagement for businesses that have identified the problems in their content operation and need someone to design a system their team can run independently.

Engagement typeProject (build)
TimelineFour to eight weeks
DeliverableWorking content system
IncludesFacilitated handover

What this engagement is for

You've identified the problems in your content operation and you need someone to design the fix. I design how content moves through the business from brief to publication, define what AI handles and what stays with the team, and build it inside the tools your team already uses so the person running it day-to-day can maintain it without ongoing external support.

How it works

1

Scoping and alignment

A session with the business owner and content lead to define what the system needs to do, what tools it lives in, and what "working well" looks like. Audit findings (mine or independent) inform the scope directly if available.

2

System architecture

I design the content workflow end-to-end: brief to publication, who's responsible at each stage, where AI is involved, and the quality checkpoints in between. Documented visually so the team can see the full operation in one view.

3

Build

I build the system inside your existing tools: templates, SOPs, governance rules, and AI prompt frameworks. Everything is built to be used by your team, not by me.

4

Facilitated handover

A 90-minute session walking the team through each component. The engagement ends at handover. The team owns the system from that point.

What you receive

A working content operations system built inside your team's tools: documented end-to-end workflow with AI integration points marked, content brief templates written for your brand and audience, editorial governance rules, AI prompt frameworks, and an SOP document your team can reference after handover.

Who this is for

Businesses with a small content team producing content with AI but without a clear system: no documented workflow, inconsistent quality standards, uncertainty about who's responsible for what. Often the natural follow-on from an audit, though some businesses arrive knowing what they need.
