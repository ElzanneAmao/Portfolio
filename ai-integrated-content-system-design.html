Build engagement

AI-integrated content system design

A build engagement for businesses that have identified the problems in their content operation and need someone to design and implement the system.

Engagement typeProject (bounded)
TimelineFour to six weeks
DeliverableWorking content system
IncludesTeam handover and documentation
The engagement

What this engagement is for

You've done the diagnosis — either yourself or through the audit. You know the gaps. Now you need someone to design the system that closes them. This engagement produces a working content operation your team can run without ongoing reliance on outside expertise.

The process

A four-phase build

01 Phase 01

Scoping call

A session to understand the business, the team, the current content workflow, and what the rebuilt system needs to achieve. This shapes everything that follows.

Phase 02

Architecture

I design the workflow logic: which stages AI handles, which need human judgment, and how content moves from brief to sign-off.

Phase 03

Build

Building the templates, prompts, review processes, and quality standards that make the system run in practice.

Phase 04

Handover

A structured handover to the team: documentation, training, and a follow-up call four weeks post-launch.

The deliverable

What you receive

A fully documented content operation designed for your team: workflow architecture with a clear map of every stage and decision point, a set of reusable prompt templates calibrated to your brand, quality review criteria so the team knows what to check and when, and a training session to make sure the people running it can actually use it.

Who this is for

Small-to-medium businesses that have done the diagnostic work and are ready to build, or that have a clear enough picture of what's wrong to skip straight to the build. The natural next step after the audit, though not a prerequisite.
