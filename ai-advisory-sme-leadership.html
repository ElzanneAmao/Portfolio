AI advisory for SME leadership

AI has made it easy for your competitors to sound exactly like you. It's also made it easy for you to sound exactly like them. The difference between the two is a strategic communications problem, and it compounds over time if nobody's watching it.

The work

What this is about

AI keeps changing. New tools, new capabilities, new ways for your content to lose the thing that made it yours. You're running a business and you don't have time to keep track of all of it, let alone figure out what applies to your situation and what doesn't. This advisory gives you scheduled access to someone with a strategic communications background who understands the AI landscape and can help you think through the decisions that affect how your brand shows up.

The fit

Is this for you

You're a founder doing your own marketing, or leading a small team that does it. AI is part of the workflow and it's working for the most part. Perhaps you've noticed that overreliance on AI content is starting to dilute the trust your audience has in your brand. That's where I come in. With a background in strategic communications and over a decade in content operations, I help you make sure the efficiency AI gives you doesn't come at the cost of the credibility you've built.

The details

What the advisory includes

01 01

Monthly calls

Two 45-to-60-minute calls per month, scheduled in advance. Time to work through decisions, review the AI operation, and set priorities for the coming weeks.

02 02

Async access

Email access between calls for questions, quick decisions, and sanity checks on anything AI-related that comes up between sessions.

03 03

Quarterly review

A written assessment at the end of each quarter: what's changed in the operation, what's working, and where to focus next. Written so you can share it with a co-founder or leadership team if needed.

Pricing is discussed after an initial conversation. This is a committed engagement with a three-month minimum — it's scoped for businesses that are serious about maintaining their brand standards as AI becomes part of the operation.
