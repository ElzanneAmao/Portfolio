AI advisory for SME leadership

A monthly retainer for business owners who need ongoing strategic input on AI decisions without hiring a full-time strategist.

Engagement typeRetainer (ongoing)
Minimum termThree months
IncludesMonthly calls + async access
DeliverableQuarterly written review

What this engagement is for

AI decisions in a small business come up irregularly and usually without warning. You need to make a call on a tool, a workflow change, or a quality concern, and you don't have anyone in-house who knows how AI and content operations intersect well enough to advise you. This retainer gives you ongoing access to that person.

What's included

Two scheduled calls per month (45–60 minutes each), async availability on email between calls, and a quarterly written review of how the AI integration is performing relative to what the business needs. Three-month minimum commitment.

Who this is for

Founders and leadership teams already using AI in their content operation who want ongoing strategic guidance without the cost of a full-time hire. Often the natural continuation after an audit or system design engagement, though neither is a prerequisite.
