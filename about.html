The throughline

I've spent sixteen years working across education, technology, communications, and hospitality, in roles that have included content strategist, marketing manager, lecturer, programme manager, and curriculum designer. The throughline isn't an industry; it's a kind of work. I tend to be brought in when an organisation needs someone who can hold both the strategic question and the operational reality at the same time, and translate between the two clearly enough that the people on either side can act.

Currently I'm the strategic lead on content and AI-integrated workflows at Think Orion, a small education marketing agency working with university and consulting-firm clients across multiple regions. The role spans onboarding research and system design, email strategy across the programme portfolio, organic social and campaign work for senior management consulting clients, and the agency's internal AI development. Alongside that, I'm building a consulting practice for small and mid-sized businesses thinking through their own AI integration.
