AI content operations audit

Everyone on your team is doing their own thing when it comes to AI content. Good on their initiative. Not great for your brand voice.

Engagement typeProject (bounded)
TimelineApproximately two weeks
DeliverableWritten audit report
IncludesHandover call
Your time~2–3 hours total
The work

What this is about

Most businesses didn't do a formal AI rollout. Everyone started using it on their own terms, and the wild west period has produced a lot of content that sounds exactly like everyone else's. You and your competitor and the shop down the street now all have a product that has "levelled up" and "isn't just offering a solution — it's promising a new start." Your brand worked too hard to get lost in that noise.

With this audit I look at your content operation across channels, compare it against your brand standards and your competitors, and write up exactly where the problems are and what to prioritise.

The process

How it works

01 Step 01

Intake and access

A short onboarding form (~30 min) walks you through what to provide: content samples across channels, team structure, tools in use, and any existing brand documentation.

02 Step 02

Discovery call

A 60-minute conversation about your business goals, your audiences, how AI ended up in the workflow, and what you think is working and what isn't.

03 Step 03

Content and competitor review

I review content across channels against your brand standards, map where AI is involved in the workflow, and run a competitor positioning review including at least one alternative competitor you may not have considered.

04 Step 04

Findings and recommendations

A written report organised by priority, leading with the most pressing finding. Each finding includes what was observed, why it matters, and a recommended change.

05 Step 05

Handover call

A 60-to-90-minute session to walk through the report and discuss what comes next.

The deliverable

What you receive

A written report of about ten pages, specific to your business. It leads with the most pressing finding (not a generic overview), includes a competitor positioning review with messaging angles you can actually use, and is clear about what you can fix yourself and what would need outside help. Written so you can act on it or hand it to your leadership team and have it make sense to them too.

Who this is for

  • Founders and owner-operators who can tell the content has changed since AI entered the picture but haven't had the time to figure out where it went wrong
  • Marketing leads at small-to-medium businesses who need an outside perspective on what's actually happening across channels
  • Leadership teams about to make a decision on AI tooling and want an honest read on the current state first

The audit is a standalone service that you can implement yourself. I can also help build your content operations so your team can work more effectively with AI.

This methodology was developed through extensive work in multichannel content operations, education marketing, and multi-brand content strategy. Pricing is scoped per engagement and discussed after an initial conversation.
